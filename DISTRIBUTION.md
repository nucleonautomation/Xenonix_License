## Online installer

The installer downloads dependencies from upstream and links to this repository for licensing information. It does not inspect a license catalog, approve package licenses or require this repository beside Setup. Package compatibility and runtime integrity checks are separate from licensing and remain in place.

Preserve notices included in installed packages. A website link is not a universal replacement for accompanying notices or license copies. This reference documents the listed releases; new or different resolved dependencies require their own current notices. It does not certify every installation as GPL-free.

## Preinstalled computers and populated environments

Delivering a computer with libraries already installed redistributes those libraries, even if they were installed with pip. Record the exact shipped software, including native libraries, OS components and vendor drivers. Supply the required notices and matching source materials, or use a source-offer mechanism permitted by the applicable license. Check the requirements for the actual license version and delivery method.

This repository currently contains notices and upstream source links. **It is not a complete corresponding-source archive, a written source offer, or certification of a complete machine image.** A package source archive may not include the source, patches and build scripts for every native component in its wheel. Verify those separately before shipping.

## Library replacement and third-party rights

Customers retain the modification, redistribution and other rights granted by each third-party license. Where required, allow replacement with interface-compatible modified libraries and reverse engineering necessary to debug those modifications. Document the replacement procedure and provide installation information where the applicable license requires it. Our proprietary terms do not override these rights.

## Maintaining this repository

Add each newly distributed version and preserve earlier pages. Keep source access available for the periods and recipients required by each license. Do not promise source availability without retaining the corresponding materials. Do not alter upstream copyright or disclaimer wording. Legal summaries help navigation; original terms govern.

Our proprietary terms should be presented through the customer agreement or other applicable acceptance process. A View Licenses button is informational and does not by itself establish agreement. Review the final customer contract for the selling entity, applicable jurisdiction, promised support and any mandatory rights before commercial use of those terms.

## Installer agreement documents

Ship the two small, matching files under Setup/Legal: LICENSE.md and TERMS.md. They are independent of the third-party repository and are displayed locally by the wizard. Keep them byte-for-byte synchronized with the corresponding published agreement revision. The final Review checkbox starts unchecked and must be selected before Install. The graphical installer retains the accepted texts and hashes locally under Log; this is evidence of a local action, not authenticated identity or authority.

Unattended installation uses a separate deployment path and does not assert that the graphical checkbox was accepted. Establish the applicable agreement separately for unattended deployments and for customers receiving supplier-preinstalled computers. Have the contracting entity, liability provisions and customer acceptance process reviewed for the relevant sales jurisdictions before using these standard terms in customer contracts.
